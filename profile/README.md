# Naibooking

### The Unified Booking and Operations Infrastructure for Modern Salons

Naibooking connects beauty and grooming customers with premier salons, stylists, and wellness professionals. We eliminate fragmented scheduling, phone tag, and double bookings by providing a seamless, real-time booking journey for customers and an all-in-one management suite for salon owners.

## How Customers Use Naibooking

Naibooking makes finding and scheduling top-tier salon services fast, reliable, and stress-free.

### 1. Discover Salons & Top Stylists
- Explore verified salons near your location with high-resolution portfolios, service menus, verified customer reviews, and clear ratings.
- Filter by exact treatment category: Hair Styling & Coloring, Skincare & Facials, Nail Care & Art, Makeup & Bridal, Spa & Wellness, and Men's Grooming.

### 2. Real-Time Slot Selection
- Select individual services or bundle multi-treatment packages.
- Choose your preferred specialist or opt for first-available professional.
- View live calendar slots with zero risk of double booking or unexpected wait times.

### 3. Transparent Pricing & Instant Booking
- Review upfront transparent pricing with no hidden charges.
- Apply discount coupons, loyalty points, and promotional codes.
- Complete instant confirmations using secure digital payment methods (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking) via Razorpay.

### 4. Effortless Appointment Management
- Receive instant booking confirmations, SMS alerts, and calendar sync.
- Receive timely push notification reminders before your scheduled appointment.
- Reschedule or cancel appointments with one tap directly inside the customer app.
- Rate your experience and leave verified feedback after your session.

## How Salon Owners and Partners Use Naibooking

Naibooking acts as an end-to-end digital operating system for salons, spas, and freelance beauty professionals, driving customer acquisition while simplifying daily operations.

### 1. Quick Onboarding & Verification
- Sign up directly at https://Naibooking.com/become-a-vendor or via the Naibooking Business App.
- Complete rapid KYC verification by submitting business address details and payout preferences to activate online booking.

### 2. Catalog & Menu Customization
- Build comprehensive service menus with custom categories, descriptions, durations, buffer times, and tiered pricing.
- Configure add-on treatments, premium product recommendations, and custom bridal or party packages.

### 3. Staff & Roster Management
- Add stylists and staff members with role-based access permissions.
- Set individual working shifts, breaks, weekly off-days, and leave quotas.
- Automatically route bookings according to stylist specialization and real-time chair availability.

### 4. Real-Time Appointment Command Center
- Receive instant push notifications on mobile and web consoles the moment a customer requests a slot.
- Accept, adjust, or propose alternative times with automated customer synchronization.
- Eliminate walk-in confusion with a unified live schedule board that combines online bookings and walk-in clients.

### 5. Point of Sale (POS), Invoicing & Automated Payouts
- Generate digital invoices, record customer payments, and track split payments effortlessly.
- Automated daily and weekly settlement directly to the salon's registered bank account.
- Manage customer memberships, package credits, and repeat client notes.

### 6. Growth Analytics & Client Retention
- Access detailed analytics on gross booking revenue, occupancy rates, and peak service hours.
- Identify top-performing stylists and trending services.
- Create custom coupon codes and targeted marketing campaigns to re-engage past clients during off-peak hours.

## Platform Architecture

Naibooking is built with an enterprise-grade, distributed architecture designed for maximum reliability and zero downtime:

- Core API Gateway: Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, and Redis session state on AWS.
- Web & Console Endpoints: Modern Next.js applications deployed globally on Cloudflare edge network.
- Mobile Applications: Cross-platform React Native / Expo apps with native iOS and Android capabilities.
- Cloud Media Storage: High-speed AWS S3 storage distributed via Amazon CloudFront.
